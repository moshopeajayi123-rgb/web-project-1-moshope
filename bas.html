Basketball

Basketball is a popular sport played by two teams of five players each. The goal is to score points by shooting a ball through the other team's hoop. The team with the most points at the end of the game wins.


History of Basketball Basketball was invented in 1891 by James Naismith, a physical education teacher in Canada who was working in the United States. He created the game to give students an indoor sport to play during the winter. Basketball became popular around the world and is now played professionally and in schools.


Rules of Basketball Each team has five players on the court. Players move the ball by dribbling or passing it to teammates. A regular shot is worth two points, a shot from beyond the three-point line is worth three points, and a free throw is worth one point. Players cannot run while holding the ball without dribbling.


Equipment Basketball players use a basketball, a hoop with a net, a basketball court, athletic shoes, and a team uniform. Some players also wear protective gear. Benefits of Basketball Basketball helps improve teamwork, coordination, balance, and communication. It also helps players develop speed, endurance, and problem-solving skills while staying active.
