History of Badminton - Badminton developed from older racket-and-shuttle games. - The modern version became popular in England during the 1800s. - The sport got its name from Badminton House in England. - Badminton became an Olympic sport in 1992.


Rules of Badminto - Players use rackets to hit a shuttlecock. - The shuttlecock must go over the net. - Players score points when their opponent fails to return the shuttlecock correctly. - Games are usually played to 21 points. - Players must stay within the court boundaries.


Equipment - Racket: Used to hit the shuttlecock. - Shuttlecock: The object players hit over the net. - Net: Separates the two sides of the court. - Court: The playing area.


Benefits of Badminton - Improves coordination. - Builds teamwork. - Helps improve balance. - Improves physical fitness. - Develops speed and reaction time.
